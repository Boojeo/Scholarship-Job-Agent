# How to use these recommendation drafts

These are **starting drafts for your recommenders**, not letters to sign as they are. Each one shows a different side of you:

| # | Recommender (suggested) | Angle | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Prof. Naeemullah Khan, KAUST Academy (your instructor and Periscope co-author) | Research ability compared with a very large, strong peer group | Every MS/PhD application, and research internships |
| 2 | Dr. A. Khanfor, Najran University (supervisor of your first-author paper) | Independence, rigour, ability to run a research project end to end | MS/PhD, especially research-heavy programmes |
| 3 | Dr. Abdulwahab Al Azeb, Programming Club supervisor, Najran University (alternative: your graduation-project supervisor, or the Dean, Prof. Khairan Rajab) | Academic record, leadership, teaching, character | MS scholarships with leadership criteria, jobs and co-op |

**Rules that protect you:**
1. Send each recommender **their** draft, the brag sheet, and your CV. Say: "Please change anything. These are only notes to save you time." Most portals ask recommenders to confirm the letter is their own, so it must be in their words.
2. Everything in `[[double brackets]]` is something **only the recommender can say** (how long they have known you, how you rank among students, a personal example). Never fill those in yourself.
3. The letter must come from **their** email or be uploaded through **their** portal link, on university letterhead and signed.
4. Ask 4–6 weeks before the first deadline. Send a polite reminder one week before.
